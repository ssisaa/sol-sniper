Callout

Callout only. Off: when the caller sells, a Since Buy under +1% keeps the trade, the pin, and the caller. At +1% or more it sells. After a negative, the recovery percent still sells once the caller is out. On: the moment the caller sells, this bot sells that token in full.

Off: Callout and OpenCopy ignore trailing and retrace. On: they use Trail starts at and drop from the high in Live Control. 0 on either of those still means the trail is off.

Default 1h. A Callout or OpenCopy trade stays open until the caller sells it, you sell it, or another exit fires. When it has been open this long (counted from the bot's buy), it sells in full. Type 2h, 3h, 5h, 1d, 2d, or minutes such as 45m. 0 turns it off.

Default 1m. Auto-buy only picks a coin opened inside this window. Type 2m, 3m, 5m, or 1h to widen it. A clicked Buy has no age limit.

Auto-buy opens a caller's new coin only when unrealized is $0.00 up to the USD top, and percentage is above 0% up to the % top. The $0.00 and 0% floors are fixed. Defaults: 5% and $100. A clicked Buy ignores this range.

Default −25. While a Callout trade is open, the next Callout coin opens only when that trade reaches this percent since buy, or when it closes. It never sells. A clicked Buy on the CallOuts page skips this wait.

    loading

    OpenHolds shows only the callers you add here and the OpenCopy trades the bot holds; Callout trades stay on CallOuts. Search a caller name, then click a match to add that caller. You can pick more than one. Coins this bot bought sit in the caller's Holding table until the trade closes, however long that takes.
